# peer-gynt

- Source: Project Gutenberg no. 66239, The Collected Works of Henrik Ibsen, vol. 4 (cache `pg66239_ibsen_peer_gynt.txt`). Ibsen, Peer Gynt, translated by William and Charles Archer. meta.year is 1892 (the first edition), as instructed; the text is that of the Collected Works (1907), which the Archers revised.
- Dropped: William Archer's introduction, the list of characters, the translators' footnotes (141 marks in the text, 5 blocks of notes, one after each act), the appendix and the Gutenberg transcriber's note. The author's note on when and where the action passes is kept as the first block.
- Parts: 38, one a scene (`a1s1`…, group `Act n`). Scenes: 3, 8, 4, 13, 10. 40,677 words, 139 pages. The script throws on any other count of acts, scenes, footnote marks or note blocks.
- The 1907 printing lost many stops and hyphens (worn plates). The transcriber supplied about thirty by conjecture (his note lists 51 changes). Here every such place was read in page images of the 1907 Scribner scan (peergyntadramat02ibsegoog) and the 1892 first edition (peergyntdramatic00ibse), with two Heinemann printings (collectedworkso04ibseuoft, ibsen04ibseuoft): 3 words, 31 stops changed to the printed reading, 16 lost stops restored, 6 hyphens, 1 compound broken at a line end.
- Left as in the file: about ten line-end commas that 1892 has and the 1907 printings lack; one stop of the transcriber's where 1892 is worded differently.
- Not compared: long dashes (the scans' text drops them), the kind of quotation mark, apostrophes.
