# adams: Henry Adams, The Education of Henry Adams (privately printed 1907; published 1918)

- Source: Project Gutenberg no. 2044 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2044), cached as `.cache/texts/pg2044_adams.txt`. A fresh download of `pg2044.txt` on 2 Oct 2026 was byte for byte the cached file. The file gives its original as the Massachusetts Historical Society's publication of 1918, and the Editor's Preface is dated September 1918; `meta.year` is 1918.
- Parts: 37. `editors-preface` (signed Henry Cabot Lodge; kept because the brief asks for it), `preface` (Adams's own, dated 1907), `c01` to `c35`; no groups. Labels give the title and dates as the book has them; the source prints them all in capitals and the script puts them in title case ("Chapter 11: The Battle of the Rams (1863)"). The script throws unless it finds the two prefaces and 35 chapters in order, each with a title of that form.
- Counts: 175,607 words, 502 pages; 900 paragraphs, 14 verse blocks (51 lines), 17 quoted blocks. No part is over 8,000 words.
- Word totals compared: the source from the heading EDITOR'S PREFACE to THE END is 175,796 words; the parts hold 175,607. The 189 between them are the headings. The script checks this sum. A separate comparison word by word (not part of the build) found the parts and the source the same and in the same order apart from the headings.
- Dropped: the Gutenberg header and licence, the title lines, the contents, the headings of the prefaces and chapters (they are the labels), the half-title before Chapter I, and THE END. Nothing follows THE END in the file but the licence (it has no index).
- Set apart: the source indents verse and quoted letters. Verse is `v` (among it an epitaph in seven lines and one line quoted alone); 13 paragraphs quoted from letters and diaries are `q`, with the date lines and salutations of two letters (`c10`, `c11`). The signature and date under the Editor's Preface and the date under the Preface are `p` blocks.
- check-text: no problems, no warnings.
- Doubt: this transcription is close to plain ASCII. The French, Italian and German quotations and one chapter title (27) have lost their accents, dashes are two hyphens, quotation marks are straight, the formula for a falling body in chapters 24 and 32 is typed with `^` for its superscript, and misprints stand as the file has them. It is exact to the file, not to the printed page.
- The first word or words of each chapter are in capitals, as in the source.
