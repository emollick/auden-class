# faust

- Source: Project Gutenberg no. 14591 (cache `pg14591_faust_taylor.txt`). Goethe, Faust, Part I, translated by Bayard Taylor; meta.year 1870 (the First Part; first edition Boston, 1870–71). The file was set from an illustrated reprint (Harry Clarke's pictures), which I did not find as a scan.
- Dropped: Taylor's preface, his poem to Goethe, the pictures and captions, six note numbers left at line ends (the notes themselves are not in the file).
- Parts: 28. `dedication`, `prelude` (labelled "Prelude at the Theatre", as the file titles it), `prologue`, then `s01`–`s25`, labelled as the book heads them. 25 scenes after the Prologue, as expected; scene 23 is prose. 34,095 words, 136 pages.
- The script throws unless: 25 scenes in order with the expected titles, 28 parts, the Dedication four stanzas of eight lines, every speaker with a speech and every speech with a speaker, the last scene ending with the Voice and its line.
- Mended (faults of the file): 4 blank lines in mid-stanza, 8 long lines broken in two, 3 words stuck on the line before, 3 lost initial words and 2 initials in capitals, 8 lost apostrophes, a speaker's name that is not in the book, a lost "(aside)", one exit and entrance on one line.
- Corrected against four printings at the Internet Archive (Boston 1871 first edition, goethetaylorfaust01; Boston 1887, cu31924026304364; Boston 1912, faust00taylgoog; London, fausttragedy00goetuoft): 26 words, 5 spellings, 62 stops, each read in page images.
- Left as in the file: contractions closed up throughout ('Tis, mustn't; Taylor prints them apart), "thousandfold", "meantime".
- LEFT UNDONE: the second pass on stops (a full stop of the file where the book has a comma; 25 found and corrected) was cut short. One place was not looked at: the stop after "fire" in scene 22 ("bachelors of youth"), where two scans' text has a comma. About 125 places where the text of a single scan differs from the file (colon or semicolon, question or exclamation mark) were not looked at in images; the other scans agree with the file there. More such full stops may remain where every scan's text is wrong together.
- Not compared: the kind of quotation mark, apostrophes.
