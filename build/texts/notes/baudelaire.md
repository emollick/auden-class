# baudelaire: Baudelaire, Intimate Papers, tr. Joseph T. Shipley (1919)

- Source: Project Gutenberg no. 47032 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/47032), Baudelaire: His Prose and Poetry, edited by T. R. Smith; the title page reads Boni and Liveright, New York, 1919 (the Modern Library's publishers). Cached as `.cache/texts/pg47032_baudelaire.txt` (a fresh download on 2 Oct 2026 was byte for byte the same). The papers are the last thing in the book.
- Translator: Joseph T. Shipley. The file says so twice: in the Contents, and in the line under the section's own heading, INTIMATE PAPERS FROM THE UNPUBLISHED WORKS OF BAUDELAIRE. The script throws if that line is not there.
- What the section holds, exactly:
  1. a translator's note of five paragraphs (515 words), untitled where it stands and called TRANSLATOR'S NOTE in the Contents;
  2. under the heading INTIMATE PAPERS, ROCKETS: 24 numbered notes, I to XXVI without IV and XIV, and one heading in capitals inside XVII (OF AIRS IN WOMAN);
  3. MY HEART LAID BARE: 73 numbered notes, I to LXXX without XXI, XXXIV, XXXVIII, XLI, XLIII, LIX and LX, and one heading in capitals inside XXXVI (PRAYER).
  One footnote (Rockets X). Nothing else: there is no third paper, and the notes on hygiene and conduct are the last numbers of My Heart Laid Bare (LXXII to LXXX).
- Parts: `rockets` (Rockets, 3,705 words) and `my-heart-laid-bare` (My Heart Laid Bare, 5,265 words): 8,970 words, 25 pages. The roman numerals are `h` blocks, level 3, as printed, gaps and all. The two inner headings are also `h` level 3, since there is no lower level; they are in ordinary capitals (Of Airs in Woman, Prayer). The script holds both lists of numerals and throws if the file's differ.
- Dropped: the translator's note; the two titles (they are the labels); the footnote and its marker; a row of asterisks inside My Heart Laid Bare XVII; italics marks. A two-line table of quantities in LXXIX is a `v` block; its second line ends in the source's ditto mark.
- Check in the script: the words of the parts are the words of the lines less the titles, the footnote and the asterisks. A separate comparison word by word found them the same and in the same order.
- Doubt: this is not the whole of the journals as they are now printed. The translator's note says the numbers are those of the manuscript's pages. Set beside the French (Gutenberg no. 13792, a later arrangement with other numbering), the numbers Shipley skips are leaves he does not give. I looked at Rockets IV and XIV and My Heart Laid Bare XXI, XXXIV, XXXVIII and XLI: lists of plans, of names and of reading, and a few maxims. Some paragraphs inside leaves he does give are missing too. In words: 3,705 and 5,265 here against 4,532 and 6,223 in that French text (which for its part lacks the hygiene leaves). "Complete" would be wrong for this version; "as Shipley gives them" is right.
- Slips of the print or the transcription are kept (a doubled full stop in Rockets V, "beast of pray" in XXIII), with one exception. The stray capital in Rockets XXVI (block 126, word 73) is a slip of the Project Gutenberg copy and not of the book: two scans of the 1919 printing read in their full text and the snippets of three more have a small letter (remake/works/baudelaire/notes.md, the finisher's point 5). On 3 October 2026 the script was given a list, SLIPS, that sets that one letter small and throws if the capital is not where it was; the note printed with the book says so.
- check-text: no problems, no warnings.
