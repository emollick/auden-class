# phedre

- Source: Project Gutenberg no. 1977 (cache `pg1977_phaedra_boswell.txt`). Racine, Phaedra, translated by Robert Bruce Boswell. The file follows the Harvard Classics, vol. 26.
- Date: the file gives none. The translation first appeared in Bohn's Standard Library (The Dramatic Works of Jean Racine, 1889–90; Phaedra is in vol. 2). meta.year is 1890, DOUBTFUL.
- Dropped: the introductory note and the list of characters. The line that sets the scene is kept as a direction at the head of Act 1.
- Parts: 5 (`act-1`–`act-5`), scene headings as `h` level 3, the persons of each scene as a `d` block under the heading. Scenes: 5, 6, 6, 6, 7 (30). 13,557 words, 50 pages. The script throws on any other count, on a speaker who is not in the scene's list, and on verse without a speaker.
- Compared with five scans at the Internet Archive: Bohn (dramaticworksje02boswgoog); the Harvard Classics first printing (continentaldram00moligoog), a later printing from the same plates (continentaldram00schigoog, continentaldram02moligoog), and the reset edition (harvardclassics26eliouoft). The file mixes readings of the two Harvard Classics settings; a reading that any of the printings has is kept.
- Corrected: 1 wrongly wrapped line; 9 words or forms and 12 stops where no scan has the file's reading (stops read in page images); 5 stops that dropped out of the Harvard Classics plates, restored from the first printing and Bohn (page images).
- DOUBT: two of those five (the commas after "falls" and "known" in Act 5) are in the first printing and Bohn but in neither later Harvard Classics printing.
- Left as in the file: "--" for the dash, straight apostrophes, ae/oe, the 'd forms.
