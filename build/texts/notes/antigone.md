# antigone

- Source: Project Gutenberg no. 31, Storr's three Theban plays (cache `pg31_sophocles_storr.txt`). Sophocles, Antigone, translated by F. Storr, Loeb Classical Library, 1912.
- Dropped: the other two plays, the translator's Argument, the list of persons.
- Parts: 5 (`p1`–`p5`), each an episode with the ode that closes it; a new part begins at the entrance of Creon, of the Guard with Antigone, of Haemon, of Teiresias. 10,118 words, 37 pages.
- The script throws if the opening direction, the five entrances, the speakers or the strophe marks are not as expected. The marks (Str. 1), (Ant. 1) are kept as `d` blocks; one antistrophe mark that the file lost is put back.
- The file has many wrong words. It was compared word for word with two scans of the Loeb volume (sophocleswitheng01soph, 1912; sophoclesstor01sophuoft, a later printing). Where both read alike against the file the printed reading is put back: 50 words and hyphens, 18 stops (the stops and the last hyphen read in page images), and one speaker's name (the file gives a speech of Creon to the Chorus).
- DOUBT, please note: in the ode to Love the file has lost the end of one line and the beginning of the next (eleven words). They are restored from the Loeb scans and so are typed in the script: the only words in my five texts that do not come from the source file.
- Left as in the file: its American spelling throughout (the Loeb spells in the English way) and "today"; two misprints of the Loeb itself in proper names.
- Not compared: the kind of quotation mark, apostrophes.
