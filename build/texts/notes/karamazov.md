# karamazov: Dostoevsky, The Brothers Karamazov, tr. Constance Garnett (1912)

- Source: Project Gutenberg no. 28054 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/28054), cached as `.cache/texts/pg28054_karamazov.txt`. A fresh download of `pg28054.txt` on 2 Oct 2026 was byte for byte the cached file. Its title page gives the Lowell Press, New York, with no date; `meta.year` 1912 is the date of Garnett's translation.
- Parts: one per chapter, 96 in all. Chapters by book: 5, 8, 11, 7, 7, 3, 4, 8, 9, 7, 10, 14, and 3 in the Epilogue. Ids `b01c01` to `b12c14`, then `epc01` to `epc03`; groups `Part I, Book I: The History of a Family` and so on, `Epilogue` for the last three. The script throws unless it finds twelve books in their four parts with exactly these counts.
- Counts: 349,291 words, 999 pages; 5,769 paragraphs, 38 verse blocks (137 lines), 11 headings, 7 quoted blocks. Two chapters are over 8,000 words and are left whole: `b05c05` (9,252) and `b06c02` (9,137).
- Word totals compared: the source from the heading PART I to THE END is 349,894 words; the parts hold 349,291. The difference is 649 words of headings out, 47 words of one footnote in, and 1 for a word rejoined (below). The script checks this sum. A separate comparison word by word (not part of the build) found the parts and the source the same and in the same order apart from those three things, and every part beginning where the source has its chapter heading.
- Dropped: the Gutenberg header and licence, the title page, the contents, the PART, Book and Chapter headings (they are the labels and groups), the nine footnote marks and eight of the nine footnotes at the end of the file, which are the translator's glosses.
- Kept from the footnotes: no. 6, on the canticles sung at a monk's burial, is Dostoevsky's own note. It stands as a `q` block after the paragraph that carried its mark (`b07c01[45]`). This is a judgement: if all footnotes should go, empty `AUTHORS_NOTES` in the script and change the count of notes kept that it expects.
- Titles: the source capitalises every word of a title ("Of A"). The small words (a, an, the, and, of, in, at, to, for, on, with) are put in lower case, except at the start of a title or of a sentence in it, to match the form asked for. Nothing else in a title is changed. Book VI, chapter 2 is titled "The Duel" in this edition.
- Set apart: verse is not indented in this file; it is told by its lines ending before the wrap width and by the two blank lines after it, and all 38 blocks were looked at. Two letters with their signatures are `q` blocks (`b09c01`, `b11c07`); other letters run in ordinary quoted paragraphs in the source and stay `p`. The headings inside Zossima's life in Book VI are `h` blocks: two of level 2 in `b06c01`, where the life begins, and (a) to (i) at level 3 through the three chapters.
- One line of the file ends inside a hyphened word (`b05c05[17]`); the two halves are joined without a space. Every other line is joined to the next with a space, as the lib does.
- Not represented: the file leaves a wider gap after a paragraph in four places (`b03c11[27]`, `b06c01[14]`, `b06c03[45]`, `b08c04[20]`). The contract has no block for a break, so the paragraphs simply follow one another.
- check-text: no problems; one warning, `b05c05[20]`, a paragraph of 9,737 characters, which is one paragraph in the source.
- Doubt: this file has no dedication, no epigraph (John 12:24) and no foreword "From the Author". The first block is the opening of Book I, chapter 1, and the last is the end of the Epilogue. If the epigraph is wanted it has to come from another edition.
- Tools: check-text does not look for a hyphen followed by a space, which is how a line broken inside a word shows after joining. A scan of all the texts on 2 Oct 2026 found no other case.
