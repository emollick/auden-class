# Augustine, Confessions: the critic's record

Piece `augustine` ("The restless heart"). Read on 3 October 2026, from
10:31 UTC, with the reading as the artist left it at 07:17. The checker
printed "no problems", "no warnings", "the printed notes are fit to
print" before I began and after every change.

How it was read. The piece was built, photographed (the first of two
runs of the picture tool, 10:31 to 10:32, "nothing reported") and its
prose printed (4,316 words). I read the whole page in the pictures at
1280 and at 390, then the prose once through before marking anything,
then `exam.md` and `art.md`, then every printed sentence twice.

The words of the work and Auden's own sentences are not written out
here. A place is named by its key in `reading.json`. A changed sentence
is given in the site's own words, and a phrase of the passage inside it
is left out and said to be left out.
