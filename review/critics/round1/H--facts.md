## Patterns

1. Source lines that do not match what was read: the wrong piece, a page range one too long, pages taken from another printing, a source that carries only part of its paragraph, pages not confirmed. Count 5. See week15.reading.3.auden.sources.1 and week15.questions.3.auden.sources.2 (also questions.4.auden.sources.2, questions.3.auden.sources.3, questions.1.auden.sources.3).
2. A year stated loosely or left pointing at the wrong thing: written or published, a spring with no year, the year of an edition. Count 3. See week15.reading.3.auden.paras.5 and week15.reading.3.auden.paras.4 (also questions.2.auden.paras.1).
3. “The list” used for the 24 required books, when the sheet holds three lists. Count 2. See week15.intro.2 and week15.questions.3.auden.paras.1.
4. Statements about the site's own contents that claim too much. Count 2. See week15.questions.4.auden.paras.4 and week15.questions.4.auden.paras.1.
5. A title not in the form its source prints. Count 1. See week15.questions.1.auden.paras.3 (repeated in sources.3).
