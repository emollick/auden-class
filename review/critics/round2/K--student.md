## Patterns

1. A name, a book, a poem or a term given without the few words a student needs (house rule 11). 9 findings. See week14.questions.4.auden.paras.1 and week14.questions.3.auden.paras.1. All nine are in the paragraphs shown after a paper is handed in. The week's introduction and the test do not have this fault.
2. A pointer whose owner is not the nearest noun, or has no noun at all (house rule 10). 8 findings. See week14.questions.4.auden.paras.3 and week14.questions.2.auden.paras.5.
3. Breaches of the house rules on form: an en-dash between months, British spelling ("counsellor", "figures"), a quotation of three lines, "himself" for a person in general. 5 findings. See week14.questions.1.auden.sources.2 and week14.questions.3.auden.paras.3.
4. What is reported from Auden stops short of the question I was asked, or the reply cannot use it. 4 findings. See week14.questions.3.auden.paras.4 and week14.questions.2.auden.paras.5.
5. A direction to another place that is not exact enough to follow. 3 findings. See week14.questions.4.auden.paras.5 and week14.questions.2.gloss.1.
6. Week 14 leaves out, or says differently, what the two earlier translation weeks say. 3 findings. See week14.making.1.rules.2 and week14.making.1.reflect.
7. The site describes its sources where I want what Auden said. 2 findings, and "in a parenthesis" in week14.questions.2.auden.paras.2 is a third case. See week14.questions.1.auden.paras.1 and week14.questions.3.auden.paras.1.
8. A verb of saying in nearly every sentence. 2 paragraphs, not entered above as findings because nothing in them misleads: week14.questions.2.auden.paras.2 (5 sentences of 6) and week14.questions.2.auden.paras.3 (6 of 7). House rule 6 lets the sentence after an attributed one go without, so "he says" in the fifth sentence of the first and "he adds" in the sixth of the second can be dropped.
9. A numeral run into the initial "K." 2 findings. See week14.questions.3.gloss.1 and week14.questions.4.text.
10. The tense of reporting changes inside one block. 1 case, not entered above: week14.questions.1.auden.paras.2 reports a printed review in the past tense, and week14.questions.1.auden.paras.3 reports another in the present, as question 2's block does throughout.

Not seen: the word list in my file lacks every entry that is a single lower-case word (for example the first half of entries 1.4, 2.2 and 5.1, and both halves of 1.5 and 3.1), so those entries are unchecked.
