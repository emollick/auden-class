## Patterns

These counts cover the whole of week 13: the 22 findings in out/J--llmspeak.md and the 5 above, 27 in all (1 must, 18 should, 8 could). A count is the number of findings in which the fault appears, and one finding may show more than one fault. Fifteen of the 27 are in the paragraphs that report Auden after a paper, eight are in the exercise, and four are in the opening paragraphs and the hints.
