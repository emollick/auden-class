## Patterns

1. A report of Auden that leaves out the one link a student needs: a term, a figure or a reason is given and not explained, or facts are given and no point drawn. 9 findings. See week13.questions.1.auden.paras.2 and week13.questions.3.auden.paras.1.
2. A pronoun or pointer with two owners or none. 8 entries, 10 places; the commonest case is the pronoun before "says" standing next to a character's name. See week13.questions.3.auden.paras.2 and week13.making.1.short.
3. Two entries that disagree or name one thing two ways (the order of events in class, the grounds for judging a ship, in or on the coffin, the form of the chapter lines, editions given or not). 5 findings. See week13.questions.2.text and week13.making.1.rules.2.
4. The reply instruction missing, incomplete or interrupted. 4 of 4 questions: none under questions 3 and 4, nothing to do if the paper agrees under question 1, a stray signpost first under question 2. See week13.questions.3.auden.paras.5 and week13.questions.2.auden.paras.4.
5. A hint that narrows the question to the site's own answer, muddles it, or is only a chapter title. 3 of 5 hints. See week13.questions.1.gloss.1 and week13.questions.3.gloss.1.
6. Marks of the revision: a verb of saying wedged in, unrelated clauses joined by "and", a name repeated at the head of three sentences. 3 findings. See week13.questions.2.auden.paras.3 and week13.questions.3.auden.paras.3.
