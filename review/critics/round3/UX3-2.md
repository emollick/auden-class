## Verdict against the brief

- Is it static? No. The late student is told what is owed and is made to do it: stretches learned in stages, cantos written out and marked, a paper of three hours with the book shut.
- Is it cheesy? No. Plain sentences and figures. A pass is one sentence and a failure is a count of words; there are no badges, cheers or streaks.
- Does it make the student learn the way the sources say Auden taught? In the parts tested, yes: five cantos by heart, written from memory against the clock and marked word for word, and the site shuts its own texts while the paper is written.
- Open at the last run: nothing of weight "must" or "should". Of the 26 old findings of those weights, 26 are fixed. Six new faults were found (one "must", five "should") and all six were put right during the test.
