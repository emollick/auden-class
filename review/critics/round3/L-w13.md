# L-w13 (week 13), last read: both lenses

Three findings. Every other marked sentence in the part is sound.

### week13.questions.1.text
- where: before the Rachel or after her
- problem: The sentence before asks whether the Rachel was the last chance, but this one allows only a place earlier or later than the Rachel, so a student who answers yes has no place to name. The paragraph shown after the paper expects that some papers will agree with Auden that it was the Rachel.
- fix: Name the last place in the book where you think he could still have turned back, whether that is the Rachel or a place before or after her, and say what stopped him.
- weight: should

### week13.questions.3.auden.paras.5
- where: Ahab’s strength and Ahab Pip’s humility
- problem: The verb is left out of the second half, so two names stand side by side and read for a moment as one name. This is the last sentence of the report on Auden, and I had to read it twice.
- fix: Repeat the verb in the second half, so that the sentence ends: "and Ahab had possessed Pip's humility."
- weight: should

### week13.questions.3.auden.paras.6
- where: If you wrote on none of the mates
- problem: This is the only instruction for the reply, and it speaks only to a student who chose no mate, though the hint under the question points students to the mates. The page does not supply the rest: it prints its general instruction for a reply only when no paragraph of the block contains the words "your reply" (site/js/views/question.js, lines 101 to 104). A student who wrote on the mates is told nothing.
- fix: Replace the paragraph with: "If you wrote on one or more of the mates, say in your reply where your account differs from Auden's and which of the two the book supports. If you wrote on none of them, say whether the fault Auden finds in the mates is in any of your three."
- weight: must
