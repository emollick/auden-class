# The pass for phones, second round: brief for its critics

The site "Fate and the Individual" is live. On 4 October 2026 the
person it was made for asked "anything else we should add or change?"
and was answered: phones first; a few small changes that had been held
back; and whether his name should stand on the site. He said yes to the
small changes and gave the words for the credit. Two critics then went
over the first part of the work (`review/phone/BRIEF.md`; their reports
are `review/phone/critic-words.md` and `review/phone/critic-eye.md`),
and what they found has been mended. Nothing of this is live yet. You
are the last readers before it is.

Read `remake/BRIEF.md` first: his words, the three rules, the house
style. The standing brief of the critics of the site's pages is
`remake/CRITIC-SITE.md`, and for words `remake/CRITIC.md`, section 2.
What he said of the first version is the thing to keep in mind on a
phone, where a page is one long column:

> "it feels like a brutal slog through the most boring webpages"

> "design, design, design! Show me visually things, make me understand trophes and rhythm and rhyme. use this as a jumping off point for people to love poetry."

> "making the analysis functional and beautiful but honoring the poems, not turning them into thematic games"

The look is settled and approved (paper, ink, two Garamonds, marks
drawn on the lines). You are not asked for a different look.

## What is asked of each critic

Your prompt says which job is yours. You edit nothing but your own
report and pictures you take into your own folder. Name each fault by
the picture and the place in it, worst first, with a line on what
would mend it. Say what should be kept. Do not copy out lines of the
works; name a line by its place.

**The critic by word.** Read every word that is new or changed, where
it stands, against section 2 of `remake/CRITIC.md` and the house
style: the credit in "Who made this" (`content/about.mjs`; pictures
`about-390-0`, `about-1280-0`), where the words "supervised and
prompted by Ethan Mollick" are his own and stay as they are, so judge
the sentence that carries them; "Contents" on the class page
(`class-scene-othello-0`, `class-up-0`); the front page's paragraph
(`site/js/home.js`, the section `home-intro`; `home-intro-390-0`);
the small words under "Read on" (`readon-*`); the class page's two
closing links (`class-end-0`); the heading of Agamemnon's plate `yoke`
(`remake/works/agamemnon/reading.json`; `plates/agamemnon-yoke-*`);
and every word the two Adams drawings print, with their folded tables
(`site/js/pieces/adams.js`; `plates/adams-dynamo-3-force-*`,
`plates/adams-born-2-book-*`). For each thing you mark give the place,
the words, the fault in a few words, and the words you would put (or
"cut").

**The critic of the drawings and the held changes.** Part A, items 1
to 3. For the two Adams drawings, at 1280, 390 and 320: is each one
clear at a glance, does every label stand clear of every other mark,
is any type under 10.5 px, does the folded table open and read well
(take a picture with it open). Check every number and name a drawing
prints against the facts of `remake/works/adams/reading.json` and
`remake/works/adams/notes.md`, and say which you checked. Compare with
`plates-live/`: say whether each drawing is better than the one it
replaces. For Blake (`sentence`, `proverbs`) and Agamemnon (`cloth` at
320, the heading of `yoke`): does each long line now turn at a place
that respects the line, at 390 and at 320, and has anything else on
those plates moved.

**The critic of the phone.** Part C, items 9 to 18, and item 19 as
far as a picture shows it. For each of the fourteen faults the first
critic by eye named (`review/phone/critic-eye.md`), say whether it is
mended, and whether the mend has made anything worse. Look hardest at
the key under its passage (does it read as belonging to the passage
it marks; is the caption that follows still easy to find; is anything
said twice), the tabs at a plate's foot (do they read as the same
tabs; are they in the way), the second passage set close, the English
after the notes on the ten Word tabs, and the menu at 320 and 360.
Compare with `plates-before/`. Then say, as the person this was made
for would: on a phone, is a piece now a pleasure to go down, or still
a slog, and where.
