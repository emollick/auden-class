# The pass for phones, second round: the critic of the phone

Working notes, saved early; the finished report replaces this text.
My pictures are in `review/phone/eye2/` (cuts of the given plates in
`eye2/s/`). Sizes are in CSS px, half of what a phone picture measures.
I edited nothing but this file and my pictures.

## First findings (plates)

  Rilke `turn`, Sentence: the key now stands straight under the marks,
  "Draw it again" under the key, the English after a rule, then the
  caption, then the numbered remarks, then the tabs again.
- Notes on plates read at full size (cuts in `eye2/s/`, 716 px wide,
  two picture px to one CSS px):
  - `rilke-panther-2-rhyme`: last German line, about 70 px of paper, the
    scheme with its arcs, the line on letters, two rows for the sounds
    that come back, a rule, the label, the English in twelve close lines
    (about 270 px, three stanzas kept apart), a rule, the caption, a
    rule, the tabs. The key is the German's and nobody's else. The
    English is plainly a second passage and reads easily.
  - `rilke-count-0-sentence`: English marked; key of three rows straight
    under it; rule; caption; rule; label; the German in seventeen close
    lines; rule; tabs. Right.
  - `baudelaire-evening-0-likeness`: the same order: marked English, the
    figure's name and plain words, three rows of colors, rule, caption,
    rule, label, the French in fourteen close lines, rule, tabs. Right.
  - `blake-sentence-0-beat`, `carmen-cartes-0-count`,

  `plates/rilke-panther-3-english-390` (in one caption, italics found in
