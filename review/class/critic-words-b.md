## Scene 6: "*Othello* on the blackboard" (scenes[5])

**[scenes[5].paras[0]]**, second sentence
- Stands: "Pearce says Auden lectured on the minds of the characters and on the society around them, and hardly touched the verse, though he told the class to read Wilson Knight, who does."
- Fault: Wilson Knight has no word of who. "Who does" is a clipped ending that leaves the reader to supply the verb, and the tense turns in mid-sentence (touched, does).
- Replace: "Pearce says Auden lectured on the minds of the characters and on the society around them, and hardly touched the verse. For the verse he told the class to read the critic Wilson Knight." ("The critic" is one added word, for the lead to confirm.)
