## Finding 1 (small)

- Place: [scenes[1].paras[1]]
- Claim: "More than half the class were graduate students, and an
  instructor of the department and two graduate assistants sat in (p. 28)."
- Fault: (d) stronger, in one word.
- The record: A, section 2.6, line 313, gives Miller p. 28 as counting an
  instructor of the English department and two graduate assistants among
  the people who were in the room, and as drawing from that mixture his
  remark that the class was nearer a seminar than a course. The record
  does not say that these three attended without being enrolled. "Sat in"
  says that they did. The record points the other way for at least one
  of them: A line 314 has Pearce (p. 132) call Millar the teaching
  assistant in the next seat, and A lines 399 and 524 have Millar enrolled
  and writing graded papers. The page itself, in
  [scenes[3].parts[0].folds[0].paras[0]], calls Millar "a teaching
  assistant" who "sat on Pearce's right" and wrote papers for the course.
- Smallest change: "and an instructor of the department and two graduate
  assistants were in the room (p. 28)."
