# Second examination of the class page, share A: part 03

Findings 7 to 12. All are small. The count and the leading findings are
at the top of the last part.

## Finding 7 (small)

- Place: [scenes[6].parts[0].folds[0].src]
- Claim: "“The Prince’s Dog,” in *The Dyer’s Hand*, pp. 198 and 204–06"
- Fault: (e) page.
- The record: the fold holds two things. `exam-others.md`, lines 237 to
  242 and 522 to 525, tested both against the kept scan of the book and
  puts the quotation on p. 198 and the sentence on the Prince and Iago on
  p. 205. C line 191 agrees for p. 198. C line 195 does put something on
  p. 204, but it is another sentence, on Falstaff and the recruits, which
  the fold does not use. Nothing in the record puts any of the fold on
  p. 204 or p. 206. The range holds the right page, so nothing is false.
- Smallest change: "pp. 198 and 205".

## Finding 8 (small, the slightest of the twelve)

- Place: [scenes[3].paras[1]], the first sentence.
- Claim: "He crossed the room, in what Pearce took for bedroom slippers,
  touched the handwritten pages on his table without looking at them, and
  went on to Descartes, who fared the same."
- Fault: (b) different, in the order of events.
- The record: in the kept scan the remark that the same holds for
  Descartes closes p. 134 and opens p. 135, before the walk. The window,
  the slippers, the table and the handwritten pages follow it, and the
  formula is taken apart after them (positions only: the first naming of
  Descartes at 8376, the walk from 8404 to 8562, Descartes again at
  8612). `exam-pearce.md`, line 360, read the page of 08:03 as saying no
  more than that Descartes fared the same; the walk was put in later,
  from its lines 783 to 786, and was put in front of that remark.
- Smallest change: "Descartes fared the same. Auden crossed the room, in
  what Pearce took for bedroom slippers, touched the handwritten pages on
  his table without looking at them, and asked which of a man’s selves …"

## Finding 9 (small)

- Place: [lede[1]], the second sentence.
- Claim: "Under each are the passages on this site that the class was
  reading."
- Fault: (f) inside the page.
- The record: the page itself. Passages stand under scenes 3 to 8, under
  the examination and under the next course. Scenes 1 and 2 of this
  share have none (scene 2 has the list to scale, which leads to whole
  pieces), and neither have the chart, the papers, the Friday evenings or
  the last scene. `exam-pearce.md`, lines 35 to 37, proposed the lede
  without this sentence.
- Smallest change: "Under most of them are passages on this site from
  what the class was reading."

## Finding 10 (small)

- Place: [contents]
- Claim: "The afternoons in order"
- Fault: (f) inside the page, and (c): an order given as known.
- The record: `exam-pearce.md`, lines 29 to 40 and 570 to 573: the first
  three scenes are set by date and the rest in the order Pearce tells
  them, and Pearce does not say whether the Thursday on Kierkegaard came
  before or after the lecture on Augustine. The page says the same in
  [caveats[1]]. Line 273 of that report notes that the claim about order
  had been taken out of the lede. This label says it again, of a list
  whose third and fourth places are in an order nobody records and whose
  last four are not afternoons.
- Smallest change: "The afternoons".

## Finding 11 (small)

## Finding 12 (small)

- Place: [scenes[1].parts[2].note], the second sentence.
- Claim: "For the eleven in black a class, a question set, a use in class
  or the examination is on record."
- Fault: (f) inside the page.
- The record: `exam-others.md`, lines 44 to 49, names the eleven, and
  `exam-pearce.md`, lines 163 to 167, shows why the words "a use in
  class" came in: Pascal is there for two remarks in the lecture on
  *Othello*. By that measure the page records one more. In
  [scenes[7].paras[5]] Auden calls Gonzalo Polonius done again, with a
  link to the piece on *Hamlet*, and *Hamlet* is on the list (A line 97)
  and is not among the eleven (A line 681 has no account of a class on
  it). So either the count or the key is off by one.
- Smallest change: "For the eleven in black a class, a question set or
  the examination is on record, or, for Pascal, the use of his terms in
  a lecture."
