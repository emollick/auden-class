## Scene 2, paragraphs

**W24 [scenes[1].paras[1]]** the paragraph, from "Miller says he talked…"
Fault: the scene is one afternoon, and this paragraph is about the term at
large ("hardly ever", "in the early meetings"). Read as the first afternoon, it
has Auden walking with his papers unopened behind him. Two paragraphs on, at the
same meeting, he sits behind the table and reads from pages. The two do not
follow.
Put, for its first sentence (with W14): "Miller says that through the fall Auden
talked and did not lecture."
Missing: the examiner should confirm that Miller, p. 28, describes the class at
large and not the first day.

**W25 [scenes[1].paras[2]]** "Chapman seldom says which of Auden's…"
Fault: "Auden's two courses" comes eleven scenes before the page says there was
a second course ([scenes[12].paras[0]]), and the lede has just said that he
taught one. "He does not say it of hers" is stiff.
Put: "Auden gave a second course in the spring, and Chapman seldom says which of
the two an answer is about. For hers he does not say."

**W21, added.** If the fright is in Frances Lewis's words and not in Chapman's,
"he says" names the wrong witness, and the sentence is "The rule frightened the
class at first, she says (pp. 508–09)." The examiner has this open
(exam2-a.01.md, inquiry 2).

## Scene 2, the note

**W26 [scenes[1].parts[1].paras[0]]** "At that meeting a gust took…"
Fault: "his pages". The man last named, in the list's label and the sentence
before it, is Pearce.
Put: "At that meeting a gust took Auden's pages off the table."
See also W40 in part 04: the pages go off the table a second time in scene 3.

**W27 [scenes[1].parts[1].paras[1]]** "He handed out the reading list."
Fault: the sentence before it has Pearce as its subject and Auden inside it.
"He" opens a new paragraph with no owner.
Put: "Auden handed out the reading list."

**W28 [scenes[1].parts[1].paras[1]]** "Tom Nolan's biography of Kenneth
Millar…"
Fault: "its length" stands three nouns away from the list (biography, class,
novels come between). "Sat in the class" echoes "sat in", which
[scenes[1].paras[1]] has just used of people who came to listen.
Put: "Tom Nolan's biography of Kenneth Millar, who was in the class and later
wrote novels as Ross Macdonald, says the length of the list drove away many who
had registered (p. 53)."

**W29 [scenes[1].parts[1].paras[1]]** "George Lefferts remembered a
prediction from…"
Fault: no word says who Lefferts is. "A prediction" announces what the next two
sentences then tell.
Put, for this sentence and the next as one: "George Lefferts, another of the
classmates Chapman heard from, remembered Auden telling them that day that as a
boy he used to drop stones down a disused coal shaft and listen for the splash,
and that once none came."
Missing: that Lefferts was one of them. The research notes say so (A, lines 453
and 549). The examiner should confirm it.

**W30 [scenes[1].parts[1].paras[2]]** "Pearce puts the cantos in December…"
Fault: "as the examination" reads first as "like the examination".
Put: "Pearce puts the cantos in December, and makes them [the
examination](#/class/exam)."

**W31 [scenes[1].parts[1].paras[3]]** "Before the class left he announced…"
Fault: "he" follows a sentence whose subject is Pearce. The address comes with
no word that it is Auden's house, and "an at-home" is an old word that needs it.
Put: "Before the class left Auden announced an at-home at his house, 1223
Pontiac Trail: Friday evenings after dinner, and everyone was welcome (Miller,
pp. 29–30)."

## Scene 2, the bars ("What he handed out, to scale")

**W32 [scenes[1].parts[2].src]** "The list is on the shelf…"
Fault: "with every book to read" reads two ways: every book that was to be read,
or every book ready to be read there. "Every" is also against the note below,
which says one play has no copy here.
Put: "The whole list is on [the shelf](#/shelf), where the books can be read."

**W33 [scenes[1].parts[2].key.taught]** "something of it is on record"
Fault: something of what, and on what record? It is the vaguest label on the
page, and it stands beside two plain ones.
Put: "on record in the class". Keep "no account found".

**W34 [scenes[1].parts[2].note]** "For the eleven in black a…"
Fault: with no comma after "black" the eye reads "black a class". "A class" and
"a use in class" are hard to tell apart. Read twice.
Put: "For the eleven in black something is on record: a class on the work, a
question set on it, a use of it in class, or a place in the examination."

**W35 [scenes[1].parts[2].note]** "Of the two Greek plays the…"
Fault: "the two Greek plays" have not been named. "The published sheet" is a
fourth name for one thing (the mimeographed reading list, the reading list, the
list, the published sheet), and the key beside it says "the list".
Put: "The list gives two Greek plays and asks for one of them, and on the copy
that has been published two books are marked "Omit" by hand."

**W36 [scenes[1].parts[2].note]** "Millar went down his own copy…"
Fault: nothing in the bars shows it. The note is eight sentences of small type,
and this one is about a student, not about the figure.
Put: cut it from the note. If it is kept, it belongs in
[scenes[1].parts[1].paras[1]], after the sentence on Nolan's biography, where
Millar has just been introduced.

**W37 [scenes[1].parts[2].note]** "From the first class to the…"
Fault: "the examinations", plural, on a page that everywhere else says "the
examination". The plural is the university's ([caveats[2]] gives its dates), and
the reader here cannot know that.
Put: "From the first class to the university's examinations was about seventeen
weeks."

**W38, for the whole share.** Millar and Miller are one letter apart, and
Miller is cited in every scene. Where Millar stands alone, give him his first
name. In this share: [scenes[1].parts[2].note] ("Millar went down…", if kept)
and [scenes[3].parts[0].folds[0].paras[0]] ("gave Millar's essays").
