## Corrections to earlier parts

- **W12.** The replacement in part 02 sets the three garments in a row, which
  is how Chapman's own phrase runs (A, line 541). The page had loosened that
  order on purpose. Put instead: "Chapman remembered Auden in blue jeans and a
  plaid shirt, with bedroom slippers on his feet, pacing as he talked and often
  stopping to look out at the street."
- **W57.** Nothing is missing. In the kept scan (p. 135) the six selves are
  given as Descartes's own case.
- **W83.** The sentence is forty-six words before the gloss. Better to break
  it at its third "that". Put, for its end: "…only while something bars its
  consummation. Against it, he wrote, stands what Christianity teaches about
  Agape, unselfish love."

## One more mark

**W88 [scenes[4].paras[0]]** "When the break was over the…"
Fault: it says again what the two lines over it have just said. The small line
says "after the recess" and the heading says "Romantic love". "Break" here is
"recess" there.
Put: cut the sentence, and begin: "Pearce says Auden lectured on romantic love
often that semester."
