# The class page, critic by word, second reading. Share A, part 02

Scene 2, "The first afternoon": the four paragraphs and the list. The note and
the bars of this scene are in part 03.

**W12 [scenes[1].paras[0]]** "Chapman remembered blue jeans and a…"
Fault: Chapman is the only man named in the sentence, so "his feet" and "he
paced" are his by grammar. "Remembered blue jeans…, and that he paced" also
yokes a noun to a clause.
Put: "Chapman remembered Auden in blue jeans, a plaid shirt and bedroom
slippers, pacing as he talked and often stopping to look out at the street."

**W13 [scenes[1].paras[0]]** "At first, he says, the class…"
Fault: "he" is Chapman and "his" is Auden, eight words apart. The verb for
Chapman changes from "remembered" to "says" between two adjoining sentences.
The citation at the end already gives the witness.
Put: "At first the class could hardly follow Auden's speech (p. 508)."
Missing: whether Chapman means the accent or the thought. If the accent, say
"could hardly make out Auden's accent".

**W14 [scenes[1].paras[1]]** "Miller says he talked and did…"
Fault: "he" may be Miller.
Put: "Miller says that Auden talked and did not lecture."

**W15 [scenes[1].paras[1]]** "His books and papers stayed unopened…"
Fault (small): told twice. Chapman has Auden pacing and stopping at the window
in the paragraph before, and Miller has him walking between the windows and the
door here. The second telling adds only the door.
Put: "His books and papers stayed unopened on the desk behind him."

**W16 [scenes[1].paras[1]]** "He kept to the ideas of…"
Fault (small): "kept" twice in one sentence, in two senses.
Put: "He stayed with the ideas of the books and hardly ever spoke of their
style, and in the early meetings he kept returning to Pascal's *Pensées*."

**W17 [scenes[1].paras[1]]** "His memoir in the *Ann Arbor*…"
Fault: every "he" and "his" of the paragraph up to here is Auden. This one is
Miller, and for a moment the memoir is Auden's.
Put: "Miller's memoir in the *Ann Arbor Observer* counts twenty-five students at
that first class."

**W18 [scenes[1].paras[2]]** "He asked the class to take…"
Fault: the man last spoken of is Miller, so "He" is Miller until the reader
corrects it.
Put: "Auden asked the class to take no notes, and three reasons are on record."

**W19 [scenes[1].paras[2]]** "In Miller's account he said they…"
Fault (small): "he said" stands at both ends of one sentence.
Put: "In Miller's account he told them they would be examined on what they
thought about the books, not on what he said."

**W20 [scenes[1].paras[2]]** "One of them, Frances Aaronson Lewis…"
Fault: "him" follows "a questionnaire he had sent", where "he" is Chapman.
Put: "One of them, Frances Aaronson Lewis, remembered Auden saying that one
person cannot really tell another anything specific, so there was nothing to
write down."

**W21 [scenes[1].paras[2]]** "The rule frightened the class at…"
Fault: the sentence before is about Lewis and her answer, so "he" has to be
looked for. "At first, he says" is also the frame of the last sentence of
[scenes[1].paras[0]]: the same build twice in one scene, for the same witness.
Put: "The rule frightened the class at first, Chapman says (pp. 508–09)." With
W13 taken, the echo is gone.

**W22 [scenes[1].paras[3]]** "At the first meeting, as Pearce…"
Fault: "he sat" follows "as Pearce tells it" and can be read as Pearce. No man
but Pearce has been named since the paragraph before.
Put: "At the first meeting, as Pearce tells it, Auden sat behind the table,
sweating, and said he would begin by reading some definitions, so that the class
would start from common ground."

**W23 [scenes[1].parts[0].items[3]]** "Imagination works whatever one
believes, as…"
Fault: it reads two ways. Imagination goes on working, whatever a person
believes. Or imagination works on whatever a person believes.
Put: "Whatever one believes, imagination goes on working, as breathing does."
Missing: this takes the first sense. The examiner should confirm it against
Pearce, p. 132.

A note on the list as a whole ([scenes[1].parts[0]]): two of the six items
open with words in quotation marks and four have none. The label says whose
they are, and the difference is clear enough. Nothing to change.
